# theCLINICS

## What it is

A family-practice-first clinic group serving Central Louisiana ("Cenla") for decades. **Two clinics** — flagship in Alexandria at 1587 N Bolton Ave and a second clinic in Pineville. **18 providers** across both sites (mix of MDs and NPs). About **90% of visits are family practice** — annual physicals, women's health, chronic-condition care, refills, and same-day sick visits via Access2Day. We also offer gastroenterology, podiatry, lab work, X-ray, bone density, and pulmonary function. **We do not have a cardiologist on staff, and we do not offer pediatrics** — primary care providers see patients from certain ages up and do not treat the specific needs of younger children.

Founded inside Cenla Family Medicine Associates.

## Register

**Brand.** This is a marketing / decision site. Visitors are evaluating whether to trust theCLINICS with their care or their family's care. The deliverable is the impression. No internal product UX (the patient portal lives elsewhere on eClinicalWorks).

## Users

**Primary — adult Cenla resident, 25–70.** Comparing local clinics on Google. Looking for a family-practice home — annual physical, kids' visits, refills, the occasional sick day — without a long drive or a long wait. Often researching on behalf of an aging parent or a child. Reads on phone first, sometimes returns on desktop. Frustrated by clinics that feel either corporate-cold or chaotically homemade.

**Secondary — referring patient.** Already established. Visiting to grab the phone number, log into the patient portal, or send the link to a friend.

**Tertiary — provider recruit / partner.** A nurse practitioner or physician scanning the about / careers surface to evaluate culture.

## What's at stake for the visitor

Trust. Healthcare decisions are high-stakes and partly emotional. The visitor is asking, often without admitting it: "Will I be treated as a person? Will the staff be competent? Will the bill make sense? Will I get an answer the same day or be told to come back next week?"

The site's job is to make the answer obvious within ten seconds: yes, yes, yes, yes.

## Strategic principles

1. **Hometown gravity.** Cenla is a real place with real geography (Red River, Pineville, Ball, Tioga, Boyce). The brand doesn't whisper about "the community" in the abstract. It names the towns. It holds the dirt.

2. **Family practice, done well.** The differentiator vs. corporate medicine is continuity — the same provider, who knows your story, manages roughly 90% of your visits. Adolescents through geriatrics. The site must make that legible.

3. **Provider-led trust.** Trust transfers from a face, not a logo. Faces and bios are central. We do not hide behind brand abstraction.

4. **Plain-English authority.** The voice is warm, direct, slightly confident. Not cute. Not corporate-academic. Not folksy. Closer to a friend who happens to be a doctor than to a hospital marketing team.

5. **Speed of answers.** Most visitors leave within 30 seconds. Phone number, hours, address, and "are they open right now" must be one glance away on every page.

## Brand voice — three physical-object words

**Solid. Clean. Local.** A galvanized-steel mailbox at the end of a long Cenla driveway, freshly painted, with the family name stenciled on it. Not precious. Not luxury. Built to last and recognizably yours.

## Anti-references — what this is NOT

- **Not corporate-hospital.** Not Ascension, Christus, HCA brand pages. We are not a system. We are a clinic with a phone number that goes to a person.
- **Not editorial-magazine.** Not a Conde Nast Traveler hotel page. Not a literary journal. Healthcare is not a luxury good and we don't aspire to feel like one.
- **Not generic-startup.** Not One Medical / Forward Health pastel-and-sans / "wellness lifestyle." We are not selling a SaaS subscription.
- **Not country-folksy.** Not "y'all come on in" wood-grain-and-cursive. Cenla is rural-adjacent, not theme-park rural.
- **Not AI-template.** Not the editorial-typographic Fraunces-and-Inter-and-italic-drop-cap aesthetic that has flooded brand sites by 2026.

## Out of scope (intentionally)

- Telehealth marketing. We don't lead with virtual care.
- HIPAA-compliant in-app messaging. Lives on the patient portal.
- E-commerce. No bookings transact on this site. **Visit requests go to the patient portal**, which already has a proven visit-request flow.
- Cardiology. We do not have a cardiologist on staff and do not advertise cardiac diagnostics.
